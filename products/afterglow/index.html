Think in angles.
See the path.

Light travels in straight lines. Mirrors change its direction. Your task is to understand the board, plan your route and guide the beam to its destination.

AFTERGLOW is built around a simple idea with room for deeper thinking: observe the arrangement, choose your moves and find the path that works.
