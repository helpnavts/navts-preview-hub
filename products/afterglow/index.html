AFTERGLOW
The birth of light.

An original laser-and-mirror puzzle game built around precision, reflection and the satisfaction of solving one more level. Developed as part of the NAVTS product portfolio.

Follow the project ↗All products ↗
/ 01 — The experience

Think in angles.
See the path.

AFTERGLOW challenges players to direct beams of light through a puzzle of mirrors. Every move matters. Every reflection changes the path.

Designed as a level-based mobile puzzle experience, it brings together clean visual language, focused mechanics and a steadily unfolding challenge.

A game by NAVTS

/ 02 — A NAVTS product

An original product within the NAVTS portfolio.

AFTERGLOW is part of the NAVTS product portfolio. It represents our interest in building thoughtful digital experiences—not a separate company or a separate top-level brand.

The game is in active development. Gameplay media, release information, support details and store links will be added as they are verified and ready to share.

/ 03 — Gameplay & release

Real puzzles. Real gameplay.

We will use genuine captures from the playable build for the website and store listing. This keeps every screenshot faithful to the game people will actually download.

Store-ready compositions can add short headlines and framing around real gameplay, but the board, controls, move counts and results will remain authentic to the released experience.
